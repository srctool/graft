# Name and logo policy

Graft's code is free software under [GPL-3.0-or-later](LICENSE). The **name "Graft" and the Graft logo are not**: the license gives no rights to them. Anyone may fork, change and share Graft, but a version they distribute as their own must use a different name and logo. This keeps users from mistaking a changed build for the official one.

| Allowed | Not allowed |
|---|---|
| Distributing unchanged official builds as "Graft" | Distributing a changed version as "Graft" or a confusingly similar name ("Graft Pro", "GraftX") |
| Saying a fork is "based on Graft" or "a fork of Graft" | Using the Graft logo or icon for a fork |
| Linux distributions packaging Graft with small build or packaging patches | Presenting a fork as official or endorsed |
| Writing about Graft: articles, tutorials, talks | Domains or app-store listings that look official |

GPL-3.0 section 7(e) allows declining to grant trademark rights, so this policy sits alongside the license without conflict.

Not sure whether your use is fine? Ask in [Discussions](https://github.com/srctool/graft/discussions) before you publish. The lead maintainer decides on the name policy.
